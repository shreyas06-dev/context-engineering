The bigger picture

Context engineering
is the enterprise discipline.

Prompt engineering is how you phrase a single request. Context engineering is how you design the entire information environment that AI operates within.

Gartner definition

"Designing and structuring the relevant data, workflows, and environment so AI systems can understand intent, make better decisions, and deliver contextual, enterprise-aligned outcomes — without relying on manual prompts."

The four pillars
01
Data curation

What information does the model receive? Where does it come from? How fresh is it?

02
Workflow integration

Where does AI sit in your processes? What decisions does it inform? What human oversight remains?

03
Environment design

What guardrails, permissions, and constraints shape what the model can and cannot do?

04
Governance & evolution

How does the system stay accurate over time? Who owns updates? How is compliance monitored?
